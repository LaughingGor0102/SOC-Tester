SOC Shift

You are the security analyst on duty. Alerts arrive one at a time. Some are real attacks, some are harmless. Decide what to do before the clock runs out.

How it works

1. Read the alert. Its plain-English story and the raw log are shown.
2. Pick one action: Safe, Suspicious or Attack.
3. You have 25 seconds per alert. Faster correct answers score more.
4. Stuck? Use a hint (costs 20 points). Win streaks add bonus points.

No experience needed: every answer explains what really happened, so you learn as you play. 8 alerts per shift, about 3 minutes.

All data is simulated. IP addresses use reserved example ranges. Nothing here touches a real network.
